Frequently Asked Questions

What is RefStack?
RefStack is a toolkit for soccer referees — helping you manage match details, track fitness, access resources, and stay organized on and off the pitch.
Which platforms is RefStack available on?
RefStack is available on iOS and Android.
How do I manage my subscription?
You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through the App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android) under your account subscriptions.
I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password" and enter your email address. You'll receive a reset link shortly.
How do I delete my account?
To request account deletion, email us at admin@refstack.app and we'll process your request promptly.
